Clinical Skills Steps

Step-by-step checklists for practising clinical examinations, procedures and history taking.

About the app

Clinical Skills Steps helps students rehearse clinical skills on their own or with a partner. Choose an examination, procedure or history guide, start the timer and tick off each step as you go. Then save the attempt and review what you missed.

For education and practice only. Not for clinical use, and not a substitute for supervised teaching, professional training or your own course materials. This app is independent and is not endorsed by any university or medical school.

Common questions

Where are my practice sessions stored?

Only on your phone, and in your own phone backups if you use them. There is no account and nothing is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes your saved sessions.

How do I delete a saved session?

Open the History screen, choose the session, and tap the bin icon in the top corner.
