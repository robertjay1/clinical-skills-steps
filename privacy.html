Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Summary

Clinical Skills Steps does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

When you save a practice attempt, the app stores it on your phone: which skill you practised, which steps you ticked, how long it took, and any reflection note you typed. Your year-of-study filter is stored the same way. None of this is sent to us or anyone else, and we cannot see it. Like other app data, it may be included in your own phone backups (iCloud, Google or computer) if you use them. It is deleted when you delete the app, and you can also delete individual sessions in the app.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not send your practice data, device identifiers or usage information to us or to anyone else.

Apple and Google

If you install the app through the App Store, TestFlight or Google Play, Apple or Google may collect information under their own privacy policies, such as crash reports and any feedback you choose to send through their testing apps. We only see what they share with developers under their terms.

Children

The app is intended for students in higher education and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
